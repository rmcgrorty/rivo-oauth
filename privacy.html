Rivo privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-01.

Rivo accesses Gmail data only for the account owner who authorized it (rivocos@gmail.com). Data is used solely to read, triage and send that owner's email on their behalf. Rivo does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train generalized AI models.

Rivo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: rivocos@gmail.com.
